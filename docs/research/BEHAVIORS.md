- **Hero:** three independently positioned and split-text animated title words, video background, video story preview, scroll cue and supporting copy. Story link opens a full-window film with pause, seek, mute and close controls.
- **Overview:** scroll-driven sticky stage. Rent/buy labels and the alternative housing introduction progress with scroll, not click tabs.
- **Benefits:** three scroll-driven panels synced to video timestamps 3.256, 7.275 and 13.128 seconds.
- **Comparison:** click-driven radio choices for years 5, 7 and 10; animated bar/value transitions.
- **Navigation:** click-driven animated menu, image/video cards, route transitions and close state. Social column removed.
- **Quick actions:** hero starts with registration. Scrolling reveals Ask Nell. Glass surface is sampled/rendered using the original WebGL renderer.
- **Registration:** living/trades/enquiry tabs; required personal/location/message fields, state options, postcode and phone formatting, trade multiselect, newsletter choice, pending/error/success states.
- **Ask Nell:** question selection opens a portrait video. Back-to-questions, play/pause, seek and mute controls retained; new local narrated answer clips and VTT captions supplied.
- **FAQs page:** original search/category/question structures retained.
- **Home builder:** original location gate, configuration choices, estimates and enquiry handoff retained.
- **Footer:** time-of-day responsive motion assets, local clock and home-builder CTA.
- **Cookies:** notice, settings dialog, preference persistence and reopening from the footer retained. No external analytics integration is added.
- **Responsive:** source media queries and fluid type calculations retained. Hero video switches to a smaller silent version below 768 pixels.
